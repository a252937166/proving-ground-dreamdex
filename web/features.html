Shock every market at once

Fixed absolute prices, bound to one block.

Set how far BTC and ETH move. The scenario fixes absolute prices for both and keeps every other held market at its reference mark, so no leg falls out of the arithmetic.

It is a canonical object with a SHA-256 id, bound to one block. After an execution the account is valued at the same prices again.

  • Fixture scenario and rounding tests
  • Fork every run binds its scenario id

Rebuild the account to the raw unit

From raw positions, marks and funding, with the bank's own rounding.

Health equity and every margin requirement are rebuilt from raw state, then compared with dreamDEX's MarginBank getter at the same block.

Any difference, a stale mark, a pool outside the bank's list or an unlisted stop blocks a recommendation instead of producing a confident wrong one.

  • Live, read-only 4 real accounts exact
  • Live, read-only 7,700 / 7,700 holders exact with floored PnL

Price every reduction on the book

Filled now, valued at the scenario, smallest cut preferred.

Each candidate, 25 to 100% of each BTC or ETH position, is filled now against dreamDEX's order book with a protective limit 50 bps beyond the touch, paying fees per fill and settling funding the way the venue does. The rest of the account is then valued at the scenario prices.

Among the candidates that meet the target, the smallest cut is preferred. Reductions that make the scenario worse are flagged, and ones that cannot be filled say why.

  • Fixture candidate tests
  • Fork the preview matched the real fills exactly

Reduce without flipping

An owner-bound guard that reverts a flip or a growth.

dreamDEX has no immediate reduce-only order. So the owner deploys a small guard, bound to their address, and grants it order placement on one pool through the OperatorPermissionsRegistry: the same grant dreamDEX's “Link a bot” writes.

The guard executes only if the live position still equals the confirmed one, and reverts the whole transaction, venue fills included, if the position would flip or grow. It holds no tokens and cannot withdraw.

  • Fork 38 / 38 Solidity tests against the real venue, with traces

Recompute from what filled

A receipt counts only once it is bound to the confirmation.

A receipt is credited only after it is bound to the saved confirmation: sender, guard, calldata, nonce, the guard's event and the taker's fills, decoded with the venue's own event definitions. Then the account is read at the receipt's block and every change has to be explained by the transaction itself.

The real remaining account is valued at the original scenario prices, beside the forecast and the account's actual health now.

  • Fixture 19 tampered receipts refused
  • Fork 6 alterations of a real receipt refused

Stop safely when anything is unclear

Journalled before sending; unknown stays unknown.

Every transaction is journalled before it is sent. A changed account invalidates a confirmation before sending. A transaction whose outcome cannot be established stays unknown, and nothing else is sent until it is settled from chain data.

Cleanup closes positions only through the guard, revokes every grant, withdraws, and is verified at one block across all 21 pools.

  • Fork 11 / 11 executor scenarios, including an RPC outage and a refused preflight
